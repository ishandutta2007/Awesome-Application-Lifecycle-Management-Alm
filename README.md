### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary
Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **Application Lifecycle Management platforms**, **open-source ALM frameworks**, and **software development lifecycle tools**. Whether you are looking for enterprise-grade commercial solutions (such as *Jira Software*, *Azure DevOps*, and *GitLab*), or self-hostable open-source alternatives (like *OpenProject*, *Taiga*, and *Keptn*), this list covers category leaders, requirements traceability, and privacy-respecting lifecycle management.

**Key Market Context:**
- **Salesforce DevOps Center is now Lifecycle Center**, bringing governance, change and release management, and real-time analytics together in one place to manage your application lifecycle .
- **SAP Cloud ALM** supports **+15K customers and partners** in implementation, operations, and service, with AI-assisted document summaries, classification for requirements, and new "In Review" status for tasks .
- **Keptn** is the **leading open-source cloud-native application lifecycle orchestration platform**, with **1,793 GitHub stars** and **244 forks** — automating SLO-driven multi-stage delivery and operations .
- **XQual** offers a **complete ALM solution** for traceability of releases, requirements, tests, and defects, compatible with **100+ automation frameworks** and available **on-premise for sovereign use** .
- **ALM and DevOps are two sides of the same coin** — ALM manages each piece of the application puzzle, while DevOps bridges the gap between development and operations teams for better collaboration and continuous delivery .

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **ALM and DevOps are complementary, not competing** — **ALM manages each piece of the application puzzle, while DevOps bridges development and operations for better collaboration and continuous delivery** . **The five ALM stages are Plan, Build, Test, Release, and Observe** .
- **Salesforce DevOps Center is now Lifecycle Center** — **governance, change and release management, and real-time analytics are now in one place**, with existing projects continuing to work under the new name .
- **Open-source ALM tools are not turnkey** — **OpenProject requires PostgreSQL and Ruby** . **Keptn requires Kubernetes** . **App Mesh requires Docker or native deployment** . **Always validate traceability, test management, and release orchestration with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment. 🔄
